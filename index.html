Authenticity

How to avoid fake supplements

1

Buy from the right seller

Prefer the brand's own store or an authorised retailer. On Amazon and Flipkart, check the seller — a big marketplace doesn't make every seller authorised.

2

Check the label

Intact seal, batch number, manufacturing and expiry dates, manufacturer or importer address and FSSAI licence number.

3

Use the brand's check

Scan QR or scratch codes on the brand's official website, not a link printed on a sticker.

4

Too cheap is a warning

A price far below every trusted retailer is a common sign of fake or expired stock.

5

Look for lab reports

Independent tests (for example Trustified, Informed Sport, NSF) apply to the product and batch tested, not the whole brand.

6

FSSAI isn't a lab test

An FSSAI licence shows the business is licensed. It doesn't prove a tub was tested for protein content.
